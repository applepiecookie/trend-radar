How it works

Where the numbers and labels on this page come from, and their limits.

Adoption stages

  • Upstream: appears at suppliers, fragrance houses, niche fairs, or in food & drink trends.
  • Lead market: already popular in Korea, Japan, China, the Middle East, the US or Europe.
  • Local early adopters: starting to be discussed by industry players, niche brands or communities in Indonesia.
  • Mainstream: visible in sales data, large surveys, or local mass brands.
  • Fading: directions the market is moving away from.

Momentum & radar score

  • Momentum counts how often a trend was mentioned by sources in the last 4 months: Rising (2+), Active (1), Quiet (0).
  • The mini chart shows mentions per month over the last 12 months.
  • Indonesia fit (1–3 dots) is Claude's initial judgement on climate, scent preferences, price and local culture. The team should review it.
  • Radar score = fit × 2 + number of sources (max 4) + mentions in the last 4 months (max 3).

Sources & updates

  • Every morning Claude searches the web: supplier releases, niche fairs, beauty media in Korea/Japan/China/the Middle East, Jakarta F&B trends, e-commerce data and consumer research.
  • New signals are added; existing signals that reappear are logged as new mentions and can move up in stage or strength.
  • Mention dates use the source's publication date; some are marked approximate (±) when the source only gives a month or year.

Limitations

  • TikTok, Instagram and Shopee are not read directly; signals come from published coverage, reports and sales data.
  • Google Trends is not covered yet. A Google Trends CSV export can be added to the data folder.
  • Early signals don't always become big. Use this radar to decide what to test first, then validate with team research, WGSN or Mintel.
