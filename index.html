      How to read this page

      Stages

      • Upstream: suppliers, fragrance houses, niche fairs, food & drink trends.
      • Lead market: popular in Korea, Japan, China, Middle East, US or Europe.
      • Local early adopters: starting to be discussed in Indonesia.
      • Mainstream: visible in Indonesian sales data or large surveys.
      • Fading: being left behind.

      Numbers

      • Momentum: mentions in the last 4 months. Rising = 2+, Active = 1, Quiet = 0. The bars show the last 12 months.
      • Fit (1–3 dots): Claude's initial judgement of fit with Indonesia (climate, scent preference, price, culture). Team should review.
      • Score = fit × 2 + sources (max 4) + recent mentions (max 3).
      • Product opportunities and the "what to create" line in each trend's detail are Claude's suggestions from the evidence, a starting point for the team, not validated concepts. Act now = early but high fit; Test next = promising, needs consumer testing; Watch = monitor first.
      • Word cloud: keywords from the trends in view, sized by sources + recent mentions, coloured by the stage of the trend that uses them most.

      Sources & limits

      • Updated three times a day (around 07:00, 12:00 and 17:00 WIB) by Claude from supplier releases, niche fairs, lead-market beauty media, Jakarta F&B trends, e-commerce data and consumer research.
      • TikTok, Instagram and Shopee are not read directly; signals come from published coverage. Google Trends is not covered yet.
      • Early signals don't always become big: use this to decide what to test, then validate.
